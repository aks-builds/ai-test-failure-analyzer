Research Caveat
Honest limitation: Search coverage is bounded by what surfaced through web search, GitHub, arXiv, and vendor blogs as of early June 2026. Low-visibility or recently published open-source projects (private repos, projects with few stars, or tools published within the last few weeks) may have been missed. Two claims survived with 2-1 adversarial votes rather than 3-0, introducing minor uncertainty about LogSage's config-file usage and GitLab Duo's exact data sources. The mabl MCP server finding relies on a single vendor blog post dated May 2026.

Bottom line: Confidence is high, not absolute. A niche private-repo tool matching all four criteria could exist without search visibility.
